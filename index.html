◇ Getting Started

From Wallet to
Spend Anywhere

Connect, fund with testnet USDC, see your unified balance across chains, then spend instantly anywhere - or let Oneliq AI keep any wallet topped up for you.

Step 01
🦊
Connect Wallet

Use MetaMask or any EVM-compatible wallet. Add Arc Testnet (chainId 5042002) and you're ready - USDC is the native gas token.

Step 02
💧
Get Testnet USDC

Claim free USDC from faucet.circle.com - Circle's official Arc Testnet faucet. The same USDC works for trade, agent, and gas.

Step 03
◆
Spend Anywhere

Open Balance, deposit once, and spend on any chain with a single signature - Auto, Single, or Manual sourcing, Batch Pay for many recipients, Consolidate to sweep everything onto one chain.

Step 04
◇
Automate with Oneliq AI

Open Oneliq AI (preview) and describe what you want in chat - it prefills the rule or transfer, you review and sign. Auto top-ups keep any wallet above your floor. Daily cap, revocable anytime.
